The Pill That Steals Lives - A Family's Journey to Discover the Side Effects of Antidepressants from Katinka Blackford Newman on Vimeo.

Katinka Blackford Newman is the author of "the Pill that Steals Lives". She had a severe adverse reaction to the antidepressant escitalopram for which she was hospitalized. She's written a memoir about it, contributed to a documentary film and fought tirelessly to warn others about the dangers of antidepressants. Katinka has done more to protect patients both in the U.S. the U.K. and abroud than the entire U.S. Federal Court system. Her book is a heartbreaking emotional journey through the failed mental health system. It reads like the Diary of Anne Frank for mental health patients many of whom have no mental illness other than trusting their doctors. it's outstanding and you should buy it off amazon. A story of resillience and overcoming adversity then moving on to help others avoid the same fate. Both Katinka Blackford Newman and Benjamin Bathen were issued the same exact medication and suffered the same adverse effects.
