Andrew Thibault filed a pro se Freedom of Information Act Request lawsuit when the U.S. Food and Drug Administration either refused to release or else released in full redacted form the Detailed Adverse Drug Event Reports of adverse reactions including the term homicide. The lawsuit took three years to complete forcing the release of the detailed narrative reports shortly after the last incident Mr. Bathen was incarcerated for. With no warning in the medication guide including the terms Homicide, Akathisia, Tardive Dyskenisia or Acute Psychosis there is no way for any patient to know the full extent of the dangerous side effects of SSRI antidepressants when they are recommended by therapists, many of whom do not have adequate medical training to discuss medication with a patient and do not perform the required monitoring.

The FDA does not perform any review of adverse drug event reports for Adults, however they did publish a review of adverse drug event reports for pediatric use of ████████. The report contains multiple accounts of children showing signs of hostility, aggression, and violent behavior after being treated with ████████

Initially the FDA did not respond to Freedom of Information at requests for detailed narrative adverse drug event reports for reactions of Homicide, Suicide, and other acts of violence where the patient was prescribed Lexapro, or Escitalopram. After about six months they agreed to send 10 reports at a time.
