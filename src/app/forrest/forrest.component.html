Forest Pharmaceuticals Inc. agreed to plead guilty to one criminal felony count of obstructing justice, one criminal misdemeanor count of distributing an unapproved drug in interstate commerce, and one criminal misdemeanor count of distributing a misbranded drug in interstate commerce. Under the plea agreement, Forest Pharmaceuticals will pay a criminal fine of $150 million and will forfeit an additional $14 million in assets.
