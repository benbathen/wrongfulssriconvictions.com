5.    I went to the psychiatrist she referred me to and started on ████████(████████) in the beginning of 2005. The adverse reactions started immediately but were never recognized as side effects. I was told that I needed to take the recommended dosage when I asked that it be reduced.

7.    I relocated to Boston, Massachusetts at the beginning of March of 2008. Dr. Jacobs is only licensed to treat patients in California, but our therapy sessions continued until September 23rd of 2008.

8.    By 2016, Wellbutrin(IC Buproprion) had driven my blood pressure up to dangerous levels. I could not explain why the incidents happened. She did ask me if I had taken any illegal or recreational drugs and I responded no as no alcohol or illegal drugs were involved.

10. In January of 2017 I began to show signs of akathisia, a medically induced syndrome that makes it hard for you to stay still caused by antipsychotics and SSRIs. My supervisors noticed that I was compulsively pacing back and forth at work and I was reprimanded for it. I was beginning to develop memory issues and periods of disorientation as well as involuntary facial grimacing(tardive dyskinesia). Because Dr. J████████ had spent four years convincing me that antidepressant medication was safe and couldnt possibly be causing any adverse effects other than weight gain or insomnia I did not know or have any reason to believe that these symptoms were being caused by the medication I had repeatedly been told would prevent mental health problems.

If she had she would have found that aggression is a MEDDRA coding term the FDA uses to aggregate reports of suicide, homicide and other acts of violence. No patient would be aware that reports as extreme as FAERs CaseID 6150282 in which a 41 year old adult male kills all of his children and commits suicide by firearm are aggregated into labeling terms such as aggression.

I called Dr. J████████ at this time because I had repeatedly been told to call my healthcare practitioner in the event of an emergency. I did not intend to issue threats to Dr. J████████ or anyone else. Im not sure why I didnt contact a different doctor.

Both homicidal and suicidal ideation are among the most frequently reported reactions. This information is easily discoverable using the FDAs online FAERs dashboard.

48. After filing the petition Ive continued my research on SSRIs and found many research papers on the effects of SSRIs by authors including Dr. David Healy, Dr. Peter Breggin, and Dr. Peter Goetzsche. Every author on the subject comments on what Dr. Breggin refers to as Medication Spellbinding(Intoxication anosognosia). He writes about why patients dont know the medication is the problem in his 2007 paper Intoxication anosognosia: The spellbinding effect of psychiatric drugs

Four related themes run through all of these cases:

1.    First, the individuals fail to perceive that they are acting in an irrational, uncharacteristic and dangerous manner.

2.    Second, they fail to identify the medication as playing any role in their drastically changed mental processes and activities.

3.    Third, they often think that the medication is helping, although sometimes they believe its ineffective, and they continue to take it as they deteriorate mentally. In the extreme, individuals suffering from medication-induced mania and psychosis believe that the drug is helping greatly and that they are better than ever.

4.    Fourth, some spellbound individuals become compulsively violent toward themselves or others, and commit bizarre acts wholly alien to their prior personalities.
